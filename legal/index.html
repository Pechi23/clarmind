Privacy Policy Terms of Service
The short version: ClarMind has no accounts and no server that stores your data. Everything you create stays on your device. We only send a tiny, non-identifying prompt (first name, zodiac sign, goal, date) to Google Gemini to generate your daily content. We never sell your data and show no third-party ads.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 June 2026

Information stored on your device only

Saved locally and never transmitted to us: your name/nickname, zodiac sign, wellness goal, meditation history, mood check-ins, streak, shields, XP, level, achievements, challenge progress, and reminder preferences. You can erase all of it any time via Profile → Reset onboarding.

Information sent to Google Gemini

To generate your daily quote, affirmation, zodiac message, tips, and weekly reflection, we send a text prompt containing your first name, zodiac sign, selected goal, the date, and (for the weekly reflection only) aggregate weekly counts such as number of sessions and minutes. This is processed under Google's Privacy Policy. We send no meditation history, moods, or account identifiers — because ClarMind has no accounts. Use the app offline to avoid sending anything; only the daily AI text will pause refreshing.

Notifications

Daily reminders, if enabled, are local notifications generated on your device.

Children's privacy

ClarMind is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Data security & changes

Your data is protected by your device's own security since it never leaves it (except the minimal AI prompt above). We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date reflects changes.
