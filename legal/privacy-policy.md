# Privacy Policy for ClarMind

**Last updated: 27 June 2026**

ClarMind ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mindfulness and meditation application. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how. We built ClarMind to be **privacy-first**: your personal data stays on your device.

## Summary

- ClarMind has **no user accounts** and **no backend server that stores your data**.
- Your name, zodiac sign, meditation history, moods, streaks, and progress are stored **only on your device**.
- To generate your daily content, we send a small, non-identifying prompt (your first name, zodiac sign, chosen goal, and the date) to Google's Gemini API. We do not send your meditation history, moods, or any other data.
- We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** show third-party advertising. We do **not** track you across apps or websites.

## Information we handle

### Information stored on your device only
The following is saved locally on your device using standard app storage and never transmitted to us:
- The name or nickname you enter
- Your zodiac sign and your selected wellness goal (sleep, stress, focus, or curiosity)
- Your meditation session history (durations, breathing patterns, timestamps)
- Your mood check-ins
- Your streak, shields, XP, level, achievements, and daily challenge progress
- Your notification and reminder preferences

You can erase all of this at any time using **Profile → Reset onboarding**, which permanently deletes it from your device.

### Information sent to Google Gemini (AI content)
To create your personalized daily quote, affirmation, zodiac message, wellness tips, and weekly reflection, the app sends a text prompt to the Google Gemini API containing:
- Your first name (as you entered it)
- Your zodiac sign
- Your selected goal
- The current date
- Aggregate weekly counts (e.g. number of sessions and minutes) for the weekly reflection only

This data is processed by Google to generate a response and is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google APIs Terms of Service](https://developers.google.com/terms). We do not send your name in a way linked to any account, because ClarMind has no accounts.

If you prefer not to send any data to Google, you can use the app without an internet connection; the daily AI content simply will not refresh, but all other features (meditation, breathing, streaks, sky, stats) continue to work.

## Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, ClarMind schedules **local notifications** on your device. These are generated on-device and are not sent through us or any third party.

## Children's privacy

ClarMind is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Data security

Because your personal data never leaves your device (except the minimal AI prompt described above), it is protected by your device's own security. We hold no servers containing your data.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, noted in the app.
