Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

ClarMind (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a mindfulness, breathing and astrology app. We built it to be privacy-first and local-first: your personal data lives on your device, and only the specific items described below ever leave it. ClarMind has no user accounts.

Summary

  • Your profile, sessions, moods, journal, streaks and progress are stored only on your device.
  • Some features send small, specific pieces of data off your device to make them work: AI content, the leaderboard, birth-chart geocoding and purchases (details below).
  • We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • There are no user accounts and no email/password.

Data stored only on your device

Saved locally (standard app storage) and never sent to us:

  • The name/nickname you enter, your zodiac sign and wellness goal
  • Birth details you optionally enter for numerology / birth chart (date, time, place)
  • Meditation and breathing session history, mood check-ins, evening-reflection journal
  • Streak, shields, XP, level, achievements, challenges, and your chat history with “Clara”
  • Your preferences (language, reminders, cues)

You can erase all of it at any time in Profile → Settings → Reset onboarding, or back it up via Backup & restore.

Data sent off your device (and why)

1. AI features — Google Gemini via our proxy

When you use AI features (daily content, the “Clara” chat, guided meditations, the mood check-in, weekly insights, numerology reading, personalized horoscope), the app sends the text needed for that feature to Google’s Gemini API through our own Cloudflare Worker proxy. Depending on the feature this may include your first name, zodiac sign, goal, the date, the day’s “cosmic energy”/moon phase, aggregate session/mood counts, or the text you type or say to Clara or the mood check-in. Our proxy only holds the API key and forwards the request; it does not store your requests on a server. Google processes the request under Google’s Privacy Policy. If you stay offline, AI content simply won’t refresh; everything else keeps working.

2. Leaderboard — Cloudflare D1

If you open the leaderboard (“Top”), the app sends your display name, zodiac sign and stats (XP, streak, minutes) plus a random, anonymous device id to our Cloudflare Worker + D1 database so the community ranking can be shown. There is no account and no email. Your device id is generated on your device and is never shown to other users. You can ask us to remove your leaderboard entry (see Contact); resetting the app also stops further updates.

3. Birth chart — OpenStreetMap Nominatim

If you generate a birth chart, the birth city you enter is sent to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service to look up coordinates. The result is cached on your device.

4. Purchases — the app store & RevenueCat

Purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store / Google Play and by RevenueCat. We receive your purchase/subscription status to unlock features — not your card or payment details.

5. Voice input

When you tap the microphone (Clara, mood check-in), your speech is turned into text by your device’s or browser’s speech-recognition. We do not record or store audio; only the resulting text is used as described in section 1.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, ClarMind schedules local notifications on your device. They are generated on-device and not sent through us.

Advertising & analytics

ClarMind shows no third-party advertising and does not track you across other apps. We may use anonymous product analytics (via PostHog) to understand which features are used and improve the app — events are tied only to your random device id, never to personal data, and include no message text or journal content. You can turn this off any time in Profile → Settings → “Share anonymous usage”.

Children’s privacy

ClarMind is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights & choices

  • Access / portability: export all your data via Backup & restore.
  • Erasure: Reset onboarding wipes your local data; contact us to remove your leaderboard entry.
  • Minimize sharing: use the app offline to avoid AI requests, and don’t open the leaderboard to avoid submitting a score.

EU/EEA users have rights under the GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, objection). Because the app is local-first, most of these you exercise directly on your device.

Data retention

Local data stays until you delete it. Leaderboard rows are kept until you ask us to remove yours or the entry is cleared. AI providers retain data per their own policies.
